Gestures & tips
  • Pan: drag empty space with one finger, or two fingers anywhere.
  • Zoom: pinch. Double-tap empty space to fit all photos.
  • Move a photo: drag it. Resize/rotate: tap it first to select it, then pinch & twist on it — or drag its corner handle. Pinching an unselected photo zooms the board.
  • Double-tap a photo to zoom to it.
  • Value studies: select a photo and tap Color to cycle Gray → 3 values → 5 values.
  • Color picker: turn it on and touch a photo. The loupe shows the hex color and value (0% black – 100% white). Picked colors collect in the tray.
  • Painting mode (lock) stops photos moving when your hand brushes the screen.
  • Install it: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen. Then you can share images from other apps straight into RefBoard.
  • Everything is stored on this device. Use Export board to back up.
